Memora

Birthday reminders · Android 13+ · GPL-3.0

Never miss a birthday, anniversary or festival again.

Memora reminds you early and again on the day, then has a heartfelt wish ready to send on WhatsApp, SMS or email, as a message or a handmade-looking postcard. It works offline and keeps everyone you add on your phone.

Remember everyone, without the effort

Add the people who matter once. Memora handles the dates, the reminders and the words.

  • Reminders that arrive on time

    One or two early reminders, from a week to a day before, and another on the morning, at the time you choose. Milestone alerts two weeks before the big ones, like an 18th birthday or a 25th anniversary.

  • A wish ready to send

    Formal, fun and family tones, or your own words, with the name and age filled in. Memora opens WhatsApp, SMS or email; you tap send.

  • Postcards drawn on your phone

    Marigold, Airmail, Rangoli, Big number and Confetti, shaped for a chat, a two-sided postcard or a WhatsApp Status.

  • Remember the little things

    An About note, gift ideas and gifts given each year, so you never give the same thing twice, and a history of every wish.

  • Home-screen widget

    Today’s events, a Wish button and what’s coming up, in large and 2 × 2 sizes.

  • Import from contacts

    Bring in the birthdays and anniversaries already saved in your phone contacts, in one go.

Festivals and lunar dates, too

Built with Indian families in mind, and useful well beyond them.

  • Festival reminders for Diwali, Ugadi, Sankranti, Eid, Christmas and more, from Google’s public holiday calendars. Pick up to two of 19 countries and 3 religious calendars.
  • A reminder on the festival morning, and optionally one a week before.
  • Lunar birthdays and anniversaries that repeat on the same Hindu tithi, such as Bhadrapada Shukla Tritiya, instead of the same calendar date. Worked out on your phone, with no internet needed.
  • A Sunday digest of the week ahead, and a Missed list so a belated wish is never forgotten.

The people you remember stay on your phone

No sign-up, no cloud, no ads and no analytics. Memora only goes online for public festival dates and to check GitHub for updates.

Read the privacy policy
  • Nothing about you leavesNames, dates, numbers and notes stay in the app’s private storage. Contacts are read only when you import or look up a number.
  • App lockFingerprint, your phone’s screen lock or a Memora PIN. While locked, Memora is hidden from screenshots and Recents, and names stay off the widget.
  • Encrypted backupsSave a backup to Drive or your phone, protected with a password if you like (AES-256).
  • You always tap sendMemora prepares the wish and opens your messaging app. It never sends anything on its own.

Install in a minute

Memora is shared on GitHub rather than an app store, so there is no store account and no store tracking.

  1. Download the APK

    Open the latest release on your phone and download the .apk file.

  2. Allow the install

    Tap the file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps, just this once.

  3. It keeps itself up to date

    Memora checks GitHub once a week, shows what’s new, and installs an update only if it matches the release’s SHA-256 checksum and signing key.

Questions

Is Memora free?

Yes. It’s free and open source under the GPL-3.0 licence, with no ads or in-app purchases.

Does it send messages automatically?

No. Memora prepares the wish and opens WhatsApp, SMS or email for you. You always tap send yourself.

Do reminders work without internet?

Yes. Reminders, wishes, lunar dates and the widget all work offline. Only festival dates and update checks need a connection.

Why are my reminders late?

Some phones (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Samsung) pause apps to save battery. If Memora’s Settings shows “Reminders may arrive late”, tap it and fix what’s marked, usually by setting battery use to Unrestricted. Memora doesn’t run continuous background tasks, so this won’t drain your battery.

How do I move Memora to a new phone?

Settings → Back up, save the file to Google Drive, then Restore on the new phone. Backups from Memora 1.x restore too.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. Download it from GitHub Releases.
