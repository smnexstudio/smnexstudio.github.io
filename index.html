Each one does a single job well, runs on your phone first, and publishes every line of its source.

Panelglass

Manga translator · Android 12+ · early development

Read manga, manhwa and manhua in your language. Panelglass erases the original lettering and redraws each balloon in place as you scroll, fully on your phone with Gemma 4 or Google Translate (ML Kit), or with Gemini if you add your own key. Its Studio letters the chapters you already have, from images, PDF, CBZ, ZIP or 7z.

Free, open source, and yours to check.

Both apps are open source on GitHub: Panelglass under Apache-2.0, Memora under GPL-3.0. Every release ships a signed APK with a SHA-256 checksum next to it, and both apps check for updates and verify them before installing.
